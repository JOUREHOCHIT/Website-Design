Cons of using 5G

5G in general gives people a lot of benefit compared to the older generations. However, there are some disadvantages.There are total of three big disadvantages of using 5G is the security problem and its signal penetration being weak.


First, security is one of the major issues from using 5G. Security problems has been troublesome for a lot of people since cellular technology is being introduced and 5G is no exception. There are quite amount of security threats in using 5G technology. Attackers can use packet injection to spy on a victim’s network and collect their personal information such as the source IP, allowing the attackers to disable devices to communicate with the core of the network. Furthermore, according to Park, authentication procedures is not required for terminals on wireless base stations. This allows attackers to have more opportunities to take actions to harm victims such as DoS(Denial of Service).

Second, the signal penetration of 5G is weak. The biggest difference between 5G and older generations is its wavelength being much shorter, meaning that the frequency and the energy used is much higher. With millimeter wave being introduced to the future, this can lead to many problems. Because 5G has a short wavelength, the range of 5G is much smaller than using 4G. It is also easy to be blocked by objects such as human, furniture and trees. Although its transfer rate and speed can be much higher, its performance drastically decreases unless the infrastructure meets the requirements of using 5G. The best method for gaining the fastest speed of 5G is to go near to the nodes. In addition, according to Niu, interference between two basic service sets could significantly degrade the network performance.



Although 5G may have potential for using for varieties of applications, its disadvantages cannot be underestimated.
