Security Problems

Security problems has been troublesome for a lot of people since cellular technology is being introduced and 5G is no exception. The following are the possible threats from using 5G:

Information Leakage

Attackers can use different methods such as paging sniffing to find informations by exploiting the broadcasting messages passed on from terminals.

IP Depletion

Attackers can use packet injection to spy on a victim’s network and collect their personal information such as the source IP, allowing the attackers to disable devices to communicate with the core of the network.

DoS(Denial of Service)

According to Park(2015), authentication procedures is not required for terminals on wireless base stations. This allows attackers to have more opportunities to take actions to harm victims such as DoS(Denial of Service).

Cost

Cost is one of the most important aspect for individual and businesses when purchasing 5G services. The cost varies with the performance of the service and its transfer rate. According to China Mobile, the 5G service cost is a lot higher than 4G services. The table below is shown to indicate their maximum cost and minimum cost.

From the table, the overall cost of 5G service is two times the 4G service, meaning that it is a lot more expensive.

Signal Penetration

5G is a millimeter wave which has higher frequency than older generations. Because its wavelength is much shorter than 4G and older generations, its signal is easy to be blocked by objects such as humans, walls and furniture, increasing the chance of getting poor performance from 5G connection.
