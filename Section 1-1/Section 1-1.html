Have you ever heard about 5G? I believe that many people have heard about 5G,but I think some of you are not completely comprehend about 5G.

The fifth-generation technology standard for internet mobile networks is known as 5G.5g expanded rapidly in 2009.South Korea and the United States were the first to launch 5G services, followed by the United Kingdom, China, Japan, and other nations. Compared with 4g, 5g can provide a more stable, faster,Wide coverage as well as lower latency network. Some people think that 4g can already meet our daily life ,why should we develop 5g.The development of 5g not only allows us to enjoy faster speeds when using the Internet but also will help to achieve the goals of telemedicine, smart logistics,autonomous driving and smart retail.For example, in the medical field, some specialists can use the Da Vinci robotic arm to connect patients from different places to perform surgery to gain treatment timeHowever, there are some other issues to address in the development of 5g.For example,the 5g standard, which is still in development, and the related large-scale communication standards will take some time to bring to market.
