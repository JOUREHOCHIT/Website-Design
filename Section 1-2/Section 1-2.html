High-speed mobile network

5G will revolutionize the mobile networking with high performance wireless network capable of supporting up to 10 to 20 GBPS of data download speed. This means that it is comparable with the fiber-optic Internet connection speed.

Mobile download speeds will be substantially faster, and responsive mobile Internet will give a strong mobile experience. Secure access to cloud storage, access to business apps, and the ability to virtually conduct demanding jobs with more processing power will all be made possible by 5G networks.


Entertainment and multimedia

A more immersive future is what 5G offers to entertaining, for example live streaming show and gaming. High-level live streaming will benefit greatly from 5G speeds, while ultra-low latency means that 5G gaming won't be constrained to high-powered computing devices. Moreover, processing, storing, and retrieving may all be done on the cloud.

Combining with the Virtual reality(VR) technology, more immersive and realistic user experience can be accomplished because the requirement on quick feedback and reaction time can be solved by the low-latency of 5G.

As 5G networks and devices become the standard, the development, quantity and sophistication of these applications are anticipated to skyrocket.


Internet of Things(IoT)

What is Internet of Things(IoT)?

The Internet of Things (IoT) describes the network of physical objects which are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies in order to interconnect and exchang data with other devices and systems over the internet.

Why 5G and Internet of Things(IoT)?

Due to its flexibility, unused spectrum availability, and low-cost deployment options, 5G is the most effective contender for the Internet of Things. Therefore, 5G networks can help IoT in various aspects.

1. Remote Surgery

Remote surgery is achievable given to the 5G network's high bandwidth and low latency.

Patients in nearby hospitals can receive prompt remote surgical treatment from remote experts by using medical robots and high-definition audio and video systems. The 5G network may also be used to execute scenarios like:

  1. Remote monitoring
  2. Intelligent diagnosis guidance
  3. Mobile healthcare
  4. Intelligent hospital administration
  5. AI-assisted diagnosis and treatment
These are significantly raising medical standard.

2. Autonomous Driving

With the help of low latency of 5G wireless networks, self-driving cars are being achievable.

Cars will be able to communicate with intelligent traffic signals, objects in their environment, and other moving vehicles. Self-driving cars must make decisions quickly in order to avoid collisions and ensure passenger safety.

3. Drone Operation

Drones are becoming more and more common for a wide variety of tasks, including entertainment, video capture, medical and emergency access, smart delivery, security, and surveillance. In a variety of applications, the 5G network will offer strong support and high-speed wireless internet connectivity for drone operation.

Humans have limited access to many places where drones can reach out and gather important information during situations like natural calamities.
