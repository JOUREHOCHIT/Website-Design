Features

The fifth-generation technology standard for internet mobile networks is known as 5G. 5G expanded rapidly in 2009. South Korea and the United States were the first to launch 5G services, followed by the United Kingdom, China, Japan, and other nations. Similar to other cellular networks 5G transmits data using a wide range of radio frequencies. Compared with 4G, 5G can provide a more stable, faster, wide coverage as well as lower latency network. Also, 5G can specify speed, capacity, coverage, encryption and security by dividing the network.

Functions

Some people think that 4G can already meet our daily life, why should we develop 5G. The development of 5G not only allows us to enjoy faster speeds when using the Internet but also will help to achieve the goals of different aspects. The total amount of linked IoT devices internationally will be at 13.8 billion by 2021, rising to 30.9 billion by 2025. 5G Ultra-Wideband is intended to assist in addressing the increasing demands for network bandwidth.

  • Smart Logistics
  • Autonomous Driving
  • Smart Retail

Concerns

However, there are some other issues to address in the development of 5G. For example, the 5G standard, which is still in development, and the related large-scale communication standards will take some time to bring to market. Also, in order to access the internet, you must purchase a new 5G-enabled smartphone.
